KeySwap.
Language tools in every app

A free little app for Windows and Mac. Select text anywhere (chat, email, documents, your browser), press a shortcut, and the text is replaced instantly. No copying and pasting into a website.

What it doesWindowsMac
Fix text typed in the wrong keyboard layoutl;ylfu → สวัสดี · ฟหกด → asdfCtrlShiftT⌃⇧T
Romanize Thai, or write English in Thai lettersสวัสดี → sawatdi · computer → คอมพิวเตอร์CtrlAltR⌃⌥R
Braille, both wayshello → ⠓⠑⠇⠇⠕CtrlAltB⌃⌥B
Morse code, both wayssos → ... --- ...CtrlAltM⌃⌥M
Translate Thai ⇄ EnglishThai → English, other languages → Thai · needs internetCtrlAltL⌃⌥L

Perfect if you're learning Thai, live in Thailand, or chat with Thai friends and keep typing with the wrong layout switched on.

Windows

Windows 10 / 11 · 64-bit · 14 MB · English interface

  1. Download and double-click. The KeySwap icon appears at the bottom right of your screen.
  2. Select text and press a shortcut from the table.
  3. Click the KeySwap icon to see the shortcuts or quit.
Download for Windows
Seeing a warning?
  1. "This file isn't commonly downloaded": click ⋯ next to the file › Keep › Keep anyway.
  2. Blue "Windows protected your PC" screen: click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. You only need to do this once.

The warning appears because the app isn't signed with a paid code-signing certificate; it doesn't mean there's a virus. The source code is public.

Mac

macOS 11 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel · 10 MB · English interface

  1. Download, unzip, drag KeySwap into Applications, and open it.
  2. Allow it once: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility › switch KeySwap on.
  3. Select text and press a shortcut. The ⇄ icon in the menu bar shows the shortcuts.
Download for Mac
"KeySwap Not Opened"?
  1. Click Done (not Move to Trash).
  2. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to "KeySwap was blocked…" and click Open Anyway.
  3. Open KeySwap again and click Open Anyway. After that it opens normally.

Shortcut: in Terminal run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/KeySwap.app

This appears because KeySwap isn't notarized by Apple (that needs a paid developer account); it doesn't mean there's a virus.

KeySwap runs on your computer. Only the translate shortcut sends the selected text to our translation service. Privacy policy.
